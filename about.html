About DoBu

Training for beginners, families and experienced students

DoBu Martial Arts provides martial arts and fitness training in a supportive environment. Members can choose from Judo, Karate, Jiu-jitsu and Muay Thai, with options for different experience levels.

The website gives visitors information about classes, the weekly timetable, membership prices, instructors and facilities before they decide to join.

Our Facilities

Facilities designed to support training and recovery.

Matted Training Area

A dedicated area for martial arts practice, drills and partner training.

Fitness Room

Equipment and space for strength, conditioning and general fitness.

Recovery Facilities

Changing rooms, showers, sauna and steam room facilities are available for members.
